A GLOBAL LEADER IN ABRASION RESISTANT COATING SYSTEMS

SDC Technologies is the recognized market leader in the development and manufacturing of premium abrasion, scratch, and chemical resistant coatings for plastics, glass, and metal substrates. Founded in 1986, we continuously innovate to deliver solutions for a wide range of markets and industrial applications.

  • Abrasion, Chemical, Impact, and Scratch Resistance
  • Compatible with A/R and Metalizing Treatments
  • Solvent-Free (100% Solids) and Solvent-Based Systems
  • Tintability and Precision Index-Matching
  • Primerless and Primer-Free Formulations Available
