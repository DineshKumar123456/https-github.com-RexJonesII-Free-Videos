Residential Excellence

A 'Home Away from Home' — Day, Weekly & Full Boarding

Ebenezer provides premium secure residential hostels inside Electronic City. Our boarding house program caters to three customized parent needs.

1. Day Boarding Option

Class extends into remedial help, complete homework supervision, nutrition dinners, and elite sport training. Leaves at 5:30 PM.

2. Weekly Boarding Option

Stays at campus hostel Monday through Friday enjoying focus tutoring and team workouts. Returns to home for family weekend bonding.

3. Full Residential Boarding

Ultimate system for cross-state & international kids. Complete 24/7 care, special dietician menus, weekend leisure trips, and personal laundry.

Boarding House Amenities

Our clean secure girls & boys dormitories are managed by senior resident wardens and secure guard protocols.

  • ✓ Anti-Allergic Rooms: Fully air-cooled well ventilated single/double sharing closets.
  • ✓ In-house Remedial Classes: Evening homework guides by actual school lecturers.
  • ✓ Nutritious Catering: Fresh multi-cuisine (North & South Indian, continental) dishes overseen by state food authorities.
  • ✓ Professional Medical Infirmary: Complete with round-the-clock ambulance, qualified doctors and nurse block.
💡 Day Boarding Benefit: Eliminate homework anxiety at home. Students board the buses with everyday chapters consolidated, worksheets cleared and dinner packed!
